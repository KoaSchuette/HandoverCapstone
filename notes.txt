ultraleap output

hand
├── type, id, confidence
├── grab_strength, pinch_strength
├── palm
│   ├── position      (x, y, z in mm)
│   ├── orientation   (quaternion)
│   ├── normal, direction, velocity, width
├── arm
│   ├── prev_joint    (elbow position)
│   ├── next_joint    (wrist position)
│   ├── rotation    (quaternion)
│   └── widith    
└── digits[5]         (thumb, index, middle, ring, pinky)
    └── bones[4]      (metacarpal, proximal, intermediate, distal)
        ├── prev_joint   (x, y, z where the bone starts)
        ├── next_joint   (x, y, z where the bone ends)
        ├── rotation     (quaternion)
        └── width


YOLO26 pose keypoints (COCO order, 17 per person)
Each keypoint: x, y (pixels) + confidence (0 to 1)
Left/right = the PERSON's left/right, not the camera's

0  nose
1  left eye
2  right eye
3  left ear
4  right ear
5  left shoulder
6  right shoulder
7  left elbow
8  right elbow
9  left wrist
10 right wrist
11 left hip
12 right hip
13 left knee
14 right knee
15 left ankle
16 right ankle

Arm joints for motion prediction: 5 to 10
